G-28 is live 🎉

Junaid Khan July 6, 2026

Notice of Entry of Appearance as Attorney or Accredited Representative - now available on Fillvisa Plus.

What's new

Fill G-28 and attach it directly to a case in Plus, so your appearance is on file right alongside the petition it supports.

Why Plus

There's no self-filed version of a G-28 - it only exists when someone is representing a client before USCIS. That puts it squarely in Plus, next to the rest of your case and client management.

What's next

More EB-1 through EB-5 forms are on the way. Follow this changelog for updates as they ship.

Have a form you'd like to see next? Let us know - we're building this around what practitioners actually need.
